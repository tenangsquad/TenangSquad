ABANG CC: 2007

Run a cozy cyber café in Malaysia, 2007.

You're Abang, owner of a tiny kedai siber. Seat the customers, keep the PCs alive, and make the daily target before closing time. Sounds easy. Then the blackout hits.

  • A real-time management game with a 3D cartoon look
  • Every perk helps, and every perk has a downside
  • Short sessions: a day takes only a few minutes
  • Progress saves automatically in your browser

How it plays

Easy to learn. Hard to keep calm.

Day 1 is a gentle tutorial. After that, the café gets busier, the PCs get moodier, and the customers get less patient.

🧑‍💻

Seat your customers

Tap a customer, pick 30, 60 or 90 minutes, then tap a glowing PC. Or double-tap to seat them in one go.

🔧

Fix what breaks

Crashes, frozen screens, a PC on fire. Tap the problem and send Abang over before the customer storms off.

🕵️

Handle the trouble

Suspicious browsing, loud videos, a fight at PC 5. Talk nicely, or accuse and risk being wrong.

⬆️

Upgrade your shop

Hit the target and choose a perk: more PCs, an aircond, a generator. Each one comes with a downside.

Why it's fun

Small team. Big attention to detail.

The kind of details you only get when you've actually spent too many late nights in a kedai siber.

🛵

Proudly local

RM1 for 30 minutes. MIRC chat. Mamak 24J next door. The jokes, the names and the vibes come from home.

⚖️

Choices with consequences

Accuse the wrong customer and your reputation takes the hit. Every perk helps and every perk costs you something.

🌅

A café that feels alive

Golden hour, night shifts, passing motorbikes, crickets and a tiny looping tune that gets moodier after dark.

🎩

Collect and keep

Clear milestone days to unlock hats for Abang. They stay yours, even if you start a brand new run.

🎚️

Your pace

Three difficulty levels: Santai, Biasa and Mencabar. Pause any time, or let the game pause itself when you switch tabs.

⚡

Zero friction

It's a web game. Open the link, click Mula Main and you're running a café. It's built without any engine or library.

On your phone too

Turn it sideways and play.

On a phone the game switches to its own landscape layout: the whole café fills the screen, with the queue and Abang's jobs tucked into the corners. Same game, same save.

Tentang Kami · About us

Sekumpulan 'almost' 30an.

Dulu bermain. Sekarang mencipta.

Tenang Squad ialah geng kawan-kawan yang dulu kerjanya "satu round lagi" sampai subuh. Rage quit, rebut PC paling hujung, bergaduh pasal siapa yang hogging. Sekarang umur dah 'almost' 30an: belakang mula sakit, tapi semangat masih level 99.

Daripada kutip markah dalam game orang lain, kami mula bina game sendiri. Bukan sebab nak jadi hebat, tapi sebab seronok. Tenang pun maksudnya santai, jadi kami buat game yang santai tapi tetap bikin hati berdebar. Dah rasa blackout dalam Abang CC, kan?

In English: a squad of almost-30-somethings. We used to play, now we make. Come and enjoy it with us.

  • Dulu pemainKami tahu rasa bila game tak adil. Sebab tu kami cuba buat yang adil.
  • Sekarang penciptaDari idea sampai butang terakhir, kami buat sendiri.
  • Santai tapi kemasNampak ringan, tapi ada pilihan sebenar di belakangnya.
  • Lebih seronok berkawanGame paling best bila ada geng. Ajak kawan, ajak famili.
